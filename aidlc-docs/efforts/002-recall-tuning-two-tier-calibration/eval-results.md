# Evaluation results — Effort 002

All numbers from completed runs of `tools/evaluate.py` on the full labelled
corpus: **29 authentic / 105 forged** (5 forgery modes × 21, generated by
`tools/make_corpus.py` from real SROIE receipts and the repo's sample
images). Calibration corpus (`testdata/calib`, sroie_021–045) and evaluation
corpus (sources sroie_000–020 + `data/`) are **disjoint at source level**.
Calibration: 12 documents, `target_fpr=0.02`.

Reading this honestly: the pipeline now detects **the forgery class that
dominates real-world fraud — a retyped amount — half the time, at 94.7%
precision, with every accusation carrying an independently verified evidence
bond**. The pixel-conservative modes (a spliced single digit, a
re-compressed-in-place patch, an erasure that doesn't break the arithmetic)
remain mostly out of reach of these probes at this calibration; per-mode
numbers are printed rather than averaged away precisely so nobody mistakes
17% overall for uniform coverage.

## What each change was for

1. **Two-tier calibration** (`verdict/calibrate.py`) — the baseline applied a
   per-document-maximum bound to individual regions: an honest page's worst
   region reaches ELA z ≈ 20 over 50–100 regions, while real forged regions
   measure 7–11. The screen tier (pooled per-region q98) restores the ability
   to notice a forged region at all; the strong tier (per-document extremes)
   remains the individually-damning bar.
2. **Motivated-region gate** (`verdict/agents/adjudicator.py::_is_motivated`)
   — screen-tier corroboration only accuses regions a forger had a reason to
   touch: money amounts (including short digit-dominated OCR misreads — a
   retyped total often loses its decimal point and reads as one digit run,
   verified on a real trace where a length cap intended to exclude phone
   numbers blocked a true forgery while the one digit-run false positive
   passed it anyway), the arithmetic-implicated field. Decorative letter text
   must meet the strong tier. This removed **all seven** intermediate false
   positives; every one was a screen-tier two-family claim on decorative text.
3. **Outlier quota in region selection** (`verdict/agents/prober.py`) —
   residual-outlier proposals are plentiful (a busy page produces a dozen)
   and were filling all region slots, crowding every money amount out of the
   probe list: a selection that never probes amounts cannot detect anything.
   Capped at 6, budget raised to 150 units / 18 regions.
4. **Duplication rules** — self-evident duplication accusation moved to the
   strong tier (receipts legitimately repeat "0.00"-style strings), and
   duplication may not corroborate the arithmetic rule (printing the total
   twice is the most common legitimate receipt layout).
5. **Blank-control exemption for flatness** (`verdict/agents/verifier.py`) —
   a synthetic grey patch is 100% flat by construction, so testing
   `flat_pixel_frac` against it made every erasure claim structurally unable
   to hold.

## Negative result, kept on the record

A **dead-grain scan** (`verdict/probes/noise.py::dead_grain_regions`) was
built to find erased fields — paper-coloured patches whose sensor grain is
far below the page's own paper median. It measures exactly what it claims
(erase fill grain ratio 0.22 vs paper 1.0) — but on already-JPEG-compressed
scans, compression itself produces natural grain-dead patches on content rows
with ratios 0.07–0.41, bracketing the forgery, and **identical regions fired
on forged images and their authentic originals**. Its proposals were removed
from claim generation; the probe remains available for high-quality inputs.
Consequence accepted: `erase_field` recall is near zero on compressed scans
unless the erasure breaks the arithmetic (this corpus's erasures target a
random amount, which is often a unit price that no reconciliation uses).
